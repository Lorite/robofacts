Viewers

This page is served by the rig, but your browser runs on the laptop — which is what lets a foxglove:// link open your Foxglove. RViz has no such scheme, so it gets a copy-paste command instead.

Live

On the rig: ros2 launch foxglove_bridge.launch.py
On the laptop: ssh -N -L 8765:localhost:8765 <trial-host>
Then in Foxglove: Open connection → Foxglove WebSocket → ws://localhost:8765

A TCP websocket, so it sidesteps the lab Wi-Fi's fragmented-UDP ceiling that makes cross-host DDS unreliable. The bridge is read-only: a browser cannot publish to the rig.

RViz instead (needs working cross-host DDS): rviz2 -d experiments/common/config/rviz/markerless_debug.rviz
