Viewing

This page is served by the rig, but your browser runs on the laptop, which is what lets a foxglove:// link open your Foxglove. RViz has no such scheme, so it gets a copy-paste command.

Live

  1. On the rig: ros2 launch <your_package> foxglove_bridge.launch.py
  2. On the laptop: ssh -N -L 8765:localhost:8765 <trial-host>
  3. In Foxglove: Open connection → Foxglove WebSocket → ws://localhost:8765

A TCP websocket, so it avoids the lab Wi-Fi's fragmented-UDP ceiling that makes cross-host DDS unreliable. The bridge is read-only: a browser cannot publish to the rig.

RViz instead (needs working cross-host DDS): rviz2 -d experiments/common/config/rviz/markerless_debug.rviz

Recorded

Every trial with a slim copy, per experiment, newest first, with the experiment's Foxglove layout beside its bags. ▶ Foxglove streams the slim bag through this UI over your tunnel (nothing is copied). It fails silently if Foxglove is not installed; use ⬇ then.

A layout is imported once per Foxglove (Layouts → Import from file): a foxglove:// link cannot carry one. The full picture of a trial (video, analyses, run log) is on its page: click its name.
