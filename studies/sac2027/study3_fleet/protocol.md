## Research question

When one agent among N (5, 10, 20) simulated agents misbehaves in one of four ways, does the experiment's declared gate, action or verify step refuse the trial, and does the trial's record name the misbehaving agent, for a fleet of nano-UAVs and for a mixed fleet of nano-UAVs and UGVs? And what does the framework cost per agent (gate time, metadata size), and what real-time factor does the simulation reach?

The design is the vault note `ai_chats/notes/Design - one misbehaving agent among N, found by declaration (SAC 2027 multi-agent study, 2026-10-15).md`, approved by Alejandro on 2026-10-15 (5 blocks). This README is the runbook's design half (`RUNBOOK.md` has the commands). The hypotheses, outcome rules and predictions below are pre-registered: frozen in one commit before the main run, and any later change is a logged deviation. The study generalizes the paper's attribution test (Section 4.3, 1 to 3 PX4 quadrotors) and its layered refusal (Section 4.2, outcomes O1 to O6) to 20 heterogeneous agents.

## Hypotheses (pre-registered)

- **H1, every fault is refused by the layer declared for it.** `dead_bridge` ends blocked at T-0 (O1) by the fleet watchdog check. `never_moves` ends aborted by the action (O3, exit 7). `stale_pose` and `wrong_mission` end aborted by a verify step (O4). `none` ends counted and valid (O5). Falsified by any faulty attempt that ends O5 or O6, or by any control attempt that ends O1, O3 or O4.
- **H2, the record names the agent.** In every refused attempt, the record (the gate log's failed item detail for O1, `abort_reason` plus `fleet_action.not_started` for O3, `abort_reason` plus `fleet_verdict.named` for O4) names exactly the injected agent and no other. Falsified by a refused attempt whose record names no agent, another agent, or more than one. Reported as the exact-attribution rate with an exact 95 % interval per fault, N and fleet.
- **H3, the cost per agent.** (a) The gate's duration does not grow with N: the slope over N is below 0.1 s per agent. (b) The trial metadata grows linearly with N (one `agents` entry and one `fleet_verdict` entry per agent): bytes per agent from a linear fit. (c) The real-time factor during the action is at or above 0.9 at N = 20 and at or above 0.95 at N = 5 and 10. Each clause falsified on its own.
- **H0.** Refusal and attribution do not depend on N (5 against 20) or on the fleet composition, pooled over the faulty attempts (Fisher exact tests).

## Pilot (2026-10-15, before the freeze)

Scratch attempts (`bags_scratch/`, never coverage, never in the tally) in the shared container (`ROS_DOMAIN_ID=71`, `GZ_PARTITION=fleet71`, other sessions' load average 1.5 to 2.5 on 16 cores), one per fault at N = 5 mixed, then the control at N = 20 mixed. The verify steps do not run in scratch mode, so they were run by hand on each bag. An attempt takes 37 s at N = 5 and 46 s at N = 20 (about 20 s when blocked), so the 180 attempts take about 1.8 h.

| attempt | layer that refused | agent named | gate | real-time factor | metadata |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `none`, N = 5 | none, both verify steps PASS | nobody | GO, 8.8 s, 5 checks | 1.000 | 11.8 kB |
| `stale_pose` (cf4) | verify step `fleet_states_track_bodies` | cf4 ("state frozen: its last stamp is 10.6 s behind the ground truth, rmse 0.790 m") | GO | 1.000 | 11.9 kB |
| `wrong_mission` (ugv1) | verify step `fleet_missions_match_declaration` | ugv1 ("peak travel 4.15 m, declared 2.0 m") | GO | 1.000 | 12.0 kB |
| `dead_bridge` (ugv1) | the T-0 gate, `watchdog_health` | ugv1 ("BLIND: ugv1 state missing") | NO-GO, 8.8 s | no bag | no bag |
| `never_moves` (cf4) | the action, exit 7 | cf4 ("agents did not start within 6.0 s of simulated time: cf4 (uav, progress 0.00 m)") | GO | 1.011 | 12.6 kB |
| `none`, N = 20 | none, both verify steps PASS | nobody | GO, 9.5 s | 0.857 and 0.864 (two attempts) | 22.7 kB |

Every agent started within 0.8 to 0.95 s of simulated time and every mission ended after 10.4 to 10.6 s of simulated time (drones peak at 1.045 m above their spawn, the UGV at 2.15 m of travel). The two 20-agent attempts also show the cost curves: the gate takes 0.7 s more at N = 20 than at N = 5 (0.04 s per agent) and the metadata grows by about 0.73 kB per agent.

Defects found by the pilot and fixed before the freeze (all inside this folder):

1. The bridge's Pose_V to TFMessage conversion of `/world/fleet/dynamic_pose/info` leaves the frame ids empty, so the action saw no body and aborted every agent (exit 7). The ground truth now comes from `fleet_ground_truth.py`, a relay over gz transport that keeps the model entries by name. The fleet watchdog also checks that every declared body appears in the ground truth by name (a content check, not only a rate check), so an empty-frame stream is refused at T-0 with its cause.
2. The two Gazebo plugins report odometry in different frames (the multicopter's in the world frame, the diff-drive's from its start), so three drones failed the state check by exactly their spawn offset and the UGV "finished" its 2 m at once. Every agent now measures its progress and reports its state relative to its own first odometry sample, and the verifier adds the declared spawn (declared offsets only, no alignment, as the paper's attribution test).
3. The frozen state of a `stale_pose` agent drifted by one origin per callback (the agent mutated the same message in place, RMSE 98 m). It now builds a new message every time, and the frozen state is really frozen.
4. A really frozen state carries a frozen header stamp, so pairing by stamp compared it with the ground truth of that same old instant and found no error. The verifier now pairs by the bag's receive time (as the paper's E2 test) and reports the staleness (the state's newest stamp against the ground truth's); more than 1.0 s names the agent.
5. The action and the relay aborted with SIGABRT at interpreter exit after a successful 20-agent mission (gz transport's teardown), which the runner reads as an aborted trial. Both leave with `os._exit` after their files are written.
6. The gate check strips whitespace from the health string (`BLIND:ugv1statemissing`), so the tally's name regex needed no trailing word boundary.

## Re-flight with the fixed Crazyflie model (deviation D5: 2026-10-23 08:25 to 10:58 UTC, Lab PC, 180 attempts)

The campaign re-flown unchanged after D5 (#150): the same campaign file, predictions, tally and seed procedure, the fixed model (drag 1.6e-06, `maximumLinearAcceleration 3.57 3.57 1.0`), on the Linux Lab PC (`execution_host=lab_pc`, `config/cyclonedds_fleet_labpc.xml`), one runner invocation (code `9f63d3a6`). Tally: `results/20261023T125830_one_bad_agent_fixed_model/`, with the before/after material in its `before_after/` folder. The 2026-10-15 folder is unchanged.

**177 of 180 attempts met their frozen prediction (179 of 180 before).** Per fault, pooled over N and fleet, before and after:

| `fault` | as predicted, before | as predicted, after | refused, after | exact attribution, before | exact attribution, after (95 % interval) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `none` | 60 of 60 | 60 of 60 | 0 | n/a | n/a |
| `stale_pose` | 29 of 30 | 30 of 30 | 30 | 29 of 30 | 30 of 30 (0.88 to 1.00) |
| `wrong_mission` | 30 of 30 | 30 of 30 | 30 | 27 of 30 | 30 of 30 (0.88 to 1.00) |

- **H1 holds again:** every one of the 120 faulty attempts was refused and every control counted (60 of 60). 117 were refused on their declared layer. The 3 exceptions are `never_moves` attempts in the mixed fleet (N = 20 twice, N = 10 once) whose injected agent was a UGV with a healthy UGV 3 m behind it (`ugv2` twice, `ugv4`): the healthy UGV drove its 2 m mission into the parked one and pushed it 0.60 to 0.61 m (`fleet_action.per_agent`, the pusher stopped at 1.59 m), so the action counted the parked UGV as started (0.2 m threshold) and did not abort, and the verify steps refused the attempt instead (O4), naming the parked UGV (mission travel 0.61 m, declared 2.0 m) and the pusher (state RMSE 0.41 to 0.46 m, its wheel odometry did not register the push). This is the layout defect of the first run's three `wrong_mission` collisions (3 m spacing), met through a new draw: in the 2026-10-15 run `never_moves` landed on a UGV only three times, always `ugv1` at N = 5, which has no UGV behind it. No Crazyflie is involved, so it is not a model effect.
- **H2:** exact attribution 117 of 120 (115 before), and the injected agent is in the record of all 120 (as before). The 3 records that named more are the 3 pushes above.
- **H0:** refusal does not depend on N or the fleet (40 of 40 at N = 5 and at N = 20, 60 of 60 per fleet). Exact attribution 40 of 40 at N = 5 against 38 of 40 at N = 20 (Fisher p = 0.49), 60 of 60 `uav` against 57 of 60 `mixed` (p = 0.24).
- **H3a holds:** gate slope 0.020 s per agent (0.039 before), medians 20.8, 20.9, 21.1 s at N = 5, 10, 20. **H3b:** 697 bytes per agent (701 before). **H3c holds at N = 5 and 10 and is falsified at N = 20, more clearly than before:** real-time factor median 0.999 and 0.990 (50 of 50 at or above 0.95 in both), and 0.657 at N = 20 with 11 of 50 at or above 0.8 (0.856 and 40 of 50 on the laptop). This is the host, not the model: on the Lab PC the same N = 20 mixed world with the old and the fixed model, hovering 30 s each, alternated twice (load average 5.8 during the old-model runs, 3.2 during the fixed ones), gave a mean reported real-time factor of 0.473 and 0.457 (old) against 0.436 and 0.420 (fixed), a difference of 0.04 against the 0.20 drop between the two campaigns (`before_after/rtf_ab.jsonl`, `rtf_ab.py`, a harness that loads the machine itself, so only the difference is meaningful). The Lab PC also ran the #149 worker's analyses during the run (load average median 4.0 at N = 20, 3.3 on the laptop).

**What the model change did to the flights** (`scripts/fleet_dynamics.py` on every bag of both runs, 1468 to 1470 healthy Crazyflies each, `before_after/dynamics_summary.json`): the drones fly a pure vertical mission, so the rotor drag never acted, before or after: tilt 0.0 deg and horizontal drift 0.0 m for every drone in both runs. The acceleration limit changed the climb: 0.3 m is reached 0.94 s after "go" (median, 0.78 s before), the drone overshoots 1.0 m by 0.175 m (median, maximum 0.197 m, before 0.093 m and 0.101 m) because it may decelerate at only 1.0 m/s^2, and it lands 10.68 s after "go" (10.46 s before). The overshoot stays inside the verify step's 0.3 m tolerance with 0.10 m of margin, and the `wrong_mission` drones peak at 2.15 to 2.19 m, far outside it. So none of the pre-registered thresholds was close to its limit with either model.

## Deviations from the design note (logged before the freeze)

- **D2, after the freeze, apparatus (2026-10-15 16:07, after 29 campaign attempts, before any result was computed):** under the container's shared CycloneDDS config (unicast only over `tailscale0`, discovery re-announced every 30 s), 3 of 9 attempts at N = 20 were refused at the T-0 gate because one or two agents' states never reached the fleet watchdog during the whole gate (`BLIND: cf15 state missing`, `cf9, cf10`, `cf15, cf17`), and in one `dead_bridge` attempt the gate's `/clock` check read 0 Hz. Every agent process was up within 1 s of the first, the recorded N = 20 bags hold every agent's streams, and the laptop was shared with another session's campaign (load average 3 to 8.5), so the cause is a lost initial discovery ping that the default interval retries only after the gate. The run was stopped between attempts (attempt 38 interrupted, X3), `config/cyclonedds_fleet.xml` was added (Cyclone's local defaults with multicast discovery, a 2 s re-announce interval and a 4 MB receive buffer), four scratch attempts at N = 20 under a load average of 8.5 to 11.3 then passed the gate 4 of 4 with the watchdog armed at once, and the run was restarted with it. The `ros_domain_id` probe now records `CYCLONEDDS_URI` per bag. **The pre-registered analysis is computed on the attempts after D2 only**; the 29 attempts before it (9 usable controls, 7 blocked at the gate of which 4 `dead_bridge` as predicted and 3 false NO-GOs at N = 20, 11 aborted on their predicted layer, 2 interrupted) are listed in the tally as `before_D2` and their 20 bags (the 9 usable controls and the 11 aborted faulty attempts) were moved to `bags_issues/before_D2/` so the campaign after D2 is complete in itself. No hypothesis, prediction, fault or threshold changed.
- **D3, after the freeze, apparatus (2026-10-15 18:45, after 15 attempts following D2, before any result was computed):** the same signature recurred once under the D2 configuration (`BLIND: cf7 state missing` in a `never_moves` attempt at N = 20 mixed, load average 9 to 10, the other eleven late agents reported by the watchdog's second tick): one agent's discovery took longer than the about 6 s the gate gave the fleet before reading the watchdog. The checklist now opens with a deliberate 12 s barrier (`topic_rate` on `/fleet/ground_truth`, `parallel: false`), so the watchdog is read about 18 s after the agents start. The gate's duration rises by 12 s at every N, which leaves H3a (its slope over N) untouched. The 15 attempts after D2 and before D3 are listed by the tally as `before_D3` and their bags moved to `bags_issues/before_D3/`; the pre-registered analysis runs on the attempts after D3. No hypothesis, prediction, fault or threshold changed.
- **D4, after the freeze, apparatus (2026-10-15 18:30, after 1 attempt following D3, before any result was computed):** with the gate barrier in place the gate passed, but in the first attempt (a control at N = 20 mixed) three agents did not start within the 6 s readiness window: the action's `/fleet/command` publisher is a fresh participant in every attempt, and under the shared load three agents had not matched it when "go" was published (latched delivery reaches them later than the check). The action now waits until every declared agent has matched its command publisher (`get_subscription_count()`, 60 s timeout, then "go" regardless) and records the wait and the matched count in `fleet_action` (`go_wait_s`, `go_matched_subscribers`). The attempt is quarantined under `bags_issues/before_D4/`; the pre-registered analysis runs on the attempts after D4. No hypothesis, prediction, fault or threshold changed.
- **Tally corrections, after the freeze, before any result (2026-10-15 18:10):** three defects in how `fleet_tally.py` reads the record, none about a hypothesis, prediction or threshold. Injections are matched to attempts by time window (the scratch attempts of the pilot and of the D2 test also append to the ledger), quarantined bags are found recursively, and the agents a layer names are read from its naming statement only (the verify step's `FAIL:` line, the action's "agents did not start" line, the gate item's detail, plus `fleet_verdict.named` and `fleet_action.not_started`), because `abort_reason` also carries one evidence line per agent and the action's list of every ground-truth name. Each has a test.
- **After the freeze, procedural (2026-10-15 15:48, before any result was computed):** the main run had been started through the agent's tool, whose client can be cut after a timeout and break the runner's output pipe mid-attempt. After attempt 2 the runner was interrupted (SIGINT to its PID during attempt 3's setup) and restarted detached inside the container. Attempt 3 (`20261015T134835_fault-stale_pose_rep01`, injection index 3) is on the ledger as `interrupted` with no bag. `fleet_tally.py` classifies an `interrupted` attempt as X3 (an exclusion, reported) instead of O2 (a stack that failed on its own), a one-line change with a test, touching no hypothesis. Attempts 1 and 2 stand.
- **D5, after the main run, apparatus (2026-10-23, #150, after #148 found the same bug in the Paper 3 stand-in):** the vendored Crazyflie model (`assets/crazyflie_model.sdf`) had `rotorDragCoefficient 8.06428e-05` on its four rotors, the value of the gz-sim x3 example for a 1.5 kg drone, and no `maximumLinearAcceleration` in its velocity controller. On the stand-in, whose model has the same mass, motors and gains, #148 measured 33 deg of pitch at a commanded 0.5 m/s (the drone reached 0.31 m/s), and with the right drag but no limit a velocity step clipped the motors and flipped the drone. The model now has drag 1.6e-06 and `maximumLinearAcceleration 3.57 3.57 1.0`, the values and sources of #148 (the real Crazyflie 2.1 Brushless leans 1.5 deg at 0.5 m/s, and 3.57 m/s^2 is g tan(20 deg), the firmware velocity controller's tilt limit), and `check_crazyflie_tilt.py --model-sdf` with its test guards it, also on the copy `fleet_prepare.py` writes. **The main run of 2026-10-15 (`results/20261015T191839_one_bad_agent/`, 180 attempts) was flown with the old model, and so were the pilot and the 55 attempts before D4.** The old results folder stays as it is. The campaign is re-flown unchanged (the same campaign file, predictions, tally and seed procedure) with the fixed model into a new results folder, on the Linux Lab PC (`--set execution_host=lab_pc`, a new level of `execution_host`), because Alejandro runs the simulated campaigns there. So the re-flight changes the host as well as the model: its real-time factor (H3c) and gate time (H3a) are measured on another machine and are not a model effect. The Lab PC's kernel also caps the socket receive buffer at 425984 bytes (`net.core.rmem_max` 212992, no sudo in the session), and the `min="4MB"` of `config/cyclonedds_fleet.xml` makes Cyclone refuse to create the domain there, so the Lab PC uses `config/cyclonedds_fleet_labpc.xml`: the same file with `min="default" max="4MB"`, the D2 discovery settings unchanged. A new factor `crazyflie_dynamics` (levels `x3_drag` for every attempt before, `fixed_148`, the names of `gz_collab_slam_registration_sim`) marks the model in the metadata, so the old attempts never cover the campaign again, and a new setup step `crazyflie_model_check` runs `check_crazyflie_tilt.py --model-sdf` on the model in every attempt (no Gazebo, under a second, a failure is an exclusion X1). The Lab PC's ledger starts empty, so the attempt index restarts at 1 and the injected agents are new draws of the same seeded procedure, not the agents of 2026-10-15. No hypothesis, prediction, fault or threshold changed. Result (section "Re-flight with the fixed Crazyflie model"): 177 of 180 as predicted, every faulty attempt refused, the 3 misses are UGV pushes of a new draw, not the model. One scratch attempt (`bags_scratch/20261023T082000_scratch_model150_check`, N = 20 mixed, control) preceded the run and its injection line was moved to `runs/fault_injections_scratch150.jsonl`, so the attempt index starts at 1.

- **H3c revised by the pilot:** the design note predicted a real-time factor at or above 0.9 at N = 20. The two 20-agent pilot attempts measured 0.857 and 0.864 with other sessions' load on the laptop (the feasibility run had measured 0.995 with the drones only climbing and no agent processes). The prediction is now at or above 0.8 at N = 20 (`config/predictions.yaml`), unchanged at N = 5 and 10.
